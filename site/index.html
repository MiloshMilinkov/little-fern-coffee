A print shop, then a coffee bar

Before it was Little Fern, this room was a print shop for forty years. When we took it over in 2019 we kept the old type drawers, the tiled floor and the big front window, and put an espresso machine where the press used to be.

There are only twelve seats, so nobody is a stranger for long. Ana runs the bar, Luka bakes, and the fern by the door is older than both of them.

Ana & Luka

Things our regulars already know

  • Dogs are welcome. There's a water bowl by the door and a jar of treats behind the bar.
  • Filter refills are free. Stay for a second cup, or a third, we won't count.
  • The window seat is first come, first served. Morning sun until about ten.
  • Laptops are fine on weekdays. Sockets along the long table. On weekends we keep it screen-free.
